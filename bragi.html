📖 Bragi's Lore

The Skald of Asgard — Bragi, god of poetry and eloquence, carries mead from Óðinn's court and turns every victory into saga. His runes on his tongue shape the stories told across the Nine Realms. Skald's Quill is his modern toolkit: brand narrative frameworks, content calendars built on Norse archetypes, storytelling templates that turn case studies into saga, and video/email scripts that don't just sell — they resonate.

Per Skáldskaparmál, Bragi is the god of poetry and eloquence, husband of Iðunn. (The specific "carries mead from Óðinn's court" detail is a minor elaboration; the mead of poetry is more directly Óðinn's.)

“I enjoin you, Bragi, and you, Bragi's son, to lead the foe of the gods in the cold course of the river.” Lokasenna 13 (Larrington, tr.) — Bragi addressed among the gods
The Business Lesson

💡 Turn every win into a saga — story compounds trust

Bragi's gift is memory made into story. Every victory in Asgard became a saga that outlived the battle. The agency lesson is the same: your results are only as valuable as the story you tell about them.

A case study is a modern saga — it proves capability, builds trust, and compounds into a brand. Most agencies do great work and stay invisible. Bragi's fix: a content system that turns each win into narrative, calendar, and script, so your reputation grows while you deliver.
