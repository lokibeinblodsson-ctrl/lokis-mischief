WHAT IT IS
----------
Mischief Ops is an automation operating system inspired by Loki, the
Norse trickster and blood-brother of Óðinn — the shape-shifter who
found the gap in every wall the gods built. Where rigid workflows
shatter at the first detour, Mischief Ops routes around the obstacle.

WHAT'S INSIDE
-------------
- Smart routing templates — send each task/lead down the right branch
  based on conditional logic, not a fixed straight road.
- Conditional logic blueprints — if/then/else automations that bend
  when reality bends.
- Escape-hatch automations — fallback paths that keep the work flowing
  when a step fails or a rule breaks.
- Make.com + n8n import-ready scaffolds you can drop into your account
  and adapt.

HOW TO USE
----------
1. Import the templates into Make.com or n8n.
2. Map your triggers (form, inbox, webhook) to the smart-routing entry
   point.
3. Set your conditional branches and escape-hatch fallbacks.
4. Deploy and let the mischief route itself.
