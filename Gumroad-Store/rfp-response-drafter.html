How It Works

From RFP to draft in minutes

No complex setup. Just paste, review, and share.

1

Paste the RFP

Copy the RFP, RFI, or questionnaire into the workflow trigger. The system parses key requirements, evaluation criteria, and submission deadlines automatically.

2

Research your knowledge base

The workflow scans your connected knowledge base for past proposals, relevant case studies, product documentation, and approved messaging that matches each requirement.

3

Draft the response

Using the best-matching content, an initial draft is generated section by section — complete with placeholders for specifics, citations to source material, and compliance mapping.

4

Shared doc + notify

A shared document is created automatically with the draft. Your team gets notified with a direct link, deadline info, and assigned review sections — ready to collaborate.
