How it works

The 3‑step dunning sequence

Each email is crafted to maximise payment while keeping the customer relationship intact.

1
Day 1 — Friendly

Gentle payment reminder

A polite, helpful email that assumes the customer simply forgot. Includes a direct payment link and a clear summary of what's due. No pressure, just a nudge — works for 60‑70% of overdue invoices.

2
Day 7 — Nudge

Gentle follow‑up with urgency

A slightly firmer tone that acknowledges the previous reminder and adds a light sense of urgency. Still courteous, but signals that payment is expected. Recovers another 15‑20% of outstanding invoices.

3
Day 14 — Final notice

Last notice with late fee

The final escalation. Clearly states the invoice is now overdue, adds the configured late fee, and warns of service interruption or collection action. Includes a last‑chance payment button and contact info.
